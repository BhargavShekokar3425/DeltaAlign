# E16 preregistration: repeated execution of near-one 5K totals

Status: preregistered after E15 and completed on 2026-10-03; see [findings](e16_findings.md). The frozen config retains its preregistration label; the execution metadata records completion. E15 supports component improvements but cannot resolve small 5K BA/WS setup-inclusive effects from E13. This experiment measures execution variability, not new graph-seed robustness or novelty.

Use the exact E13 jobs: 5K BA/WS, seeds 61–65, 1% noise, three shared-latent 0.1% updates, full and persistent compiled methods. Select BA/WS because their E13 median total ratios were below 1.05; ER's larger effect is outside this narrow question. Retain both selected families and every seed. Run three fresh execution rounds per fixed job, 60 sequential fresh workers and 30 paired executions. E13 historical measurements remain context, not a fourth repeat in these statistics.

Freeze E13 graph/edit generation, descriptors, normalization, cache initialization, numerical kernels, matrix release lifecycle, buffer fingerprints, validation and RSS scope. Create a new driver invoking frozen E13 workers with explicit seed/config settings; do not edit historical kernels. Check every setup/update fingerprint and objective against the paired worker and saved E13 fixed-job fingerprints (120 paired execution snapshots). Any failure stops the comparison. Inspect headroom each round and retain all actual initialization costs, whole-worker RSS and stage/update/total seconds.

Alternate first method by family/seed/round parity, balanced across the 30 pairs. Record order, start/end timestamps, CPU/software/source/config identity and inherited numerical-library thread settings. Run sequentially with no other deliberate benchmark workload. No dropped first round or favorable-repeat selection, extra warmup exclusion, timing-driven stopping, thread tuning or noise/budget change. Three fresh processes per method/job are execution repetitions; five seeds per topology are the independent graph/edit bundles.

For each fixed seed/family, report all three paired stage/update/setup-inclusive/RSS ratios and their median/range. Then report distributions across the five per-seed medians separately by topology. Do not pool 15 executions as independent graph seeds or invent significance from these counts. Include raw seconds and ratio consistency with method order. Timing spread and effects close to one constrain claims even if the point estimate is positive; no arbitrary effect threshold changes after results are seen.

Component progress remains valid independently of the total result. If total effects are within observed run variability, state that a robust total win remains unsupported. If paired effects persist across fixed-job repeats and seeds, scope the evidence to these workloads/machine; small savings do not establish broad scalability or better accuracy. Retain E13 memory regressions and E14 novelty limitations. No 10K, long stream, real-data or CPU decomposition claim follows.

Deliver `experiments/e16_timing.py`, summarizer, raw/metadata/aggregate/figure artifacts and `docs/e16_findings.md`; update paper readiness and choose the next research task from the full result.
