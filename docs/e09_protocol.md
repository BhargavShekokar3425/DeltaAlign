# E09: short persistent-state streams

Pre-run specification: fresh seeds 45–49, 1K BA attachment 3, frozen two-hop descriptor and corrected initial scale. Forty streams: two initial noise levels, two protocols, two requested fractions (0.1%/1%) and five seed bundles. Each stream has ten successive mixed batches. Per-graph edit count is rounded from initial combined edges and stays fixed; record realized fractions using current pre-batch combined edges. Independent source/target step seeds come from distinct SeedSequence branches. Shared-latent updates preserve the initial observation mask at every step. Hidden truth enters generation/evaluation only.

Methods: persistent selective + warm assignment, persistent selective + SciPy, full descriptor/cost recomputation + SciPy, and keep-initial. Each active method initializes its own required state with an actual timed pipeline. Cached initial features supply normalization/costs directly, avoiding diagnostic duplicate initialization. Keep-initial pays the full initial solve and performs no subsequent assignment work. Initial optima can differ under ties; log agreement and compare objective correctness. No solver switching or threshold tuning.

Update timings begin from already updated graphs. Persistent caches and warm dual state are mutated; no reset-only cache/state clone. Dense cost refresh and warm repair's necessary cost copies remain timed. Full SciPy runs first; selective method order alternates. Log feature/dirty support time, cost time, solver time, total update time, validation time separately, cumulative update time, and initialization plus updates. Generation/batch application is separately timed and excluded from assignment pipeline ratios, as are quality evaluation and validation. This is not full application latency.

Every step must have exact full-oracle feature, dirty-set and refreshed cost equality. Selective SciPy must return the full SciPy mapping; warm assignment must pass global dual certificates and match optimal objective. Preserve old graphs until maintenance completes. Record per-step NC/S3, initial and previous-map churn, actual edit counts, mask size, support, cost work and assignment search. Aggregate cumulative costs and stage totals per seed/stream before reporting median/IQR/ranges; ten correlated batches do not provide ten independent samples.

Decision: test sustained exact graph maintenance, method-specific setup amortization and stage improvements. Component improvements count as positive outcomes even if initialization-inclusive whole-pipeline speedup is absent; report both clearly. Ten steps are a pilot, not long-stream robustness, crossover proof or a break-even guarantee. Dense memory, fixed topology and validation-only certificates remain limits. Adaptive compiled fallback still requires warm dual-state restoration; no parallel or solver-novelty claim.
